Notes:

The xpub-plus-child-private-key compromise claim is central to safe handling and comes from the Implications section — worth extra scrutiny. Level was set to intermediate because extended-key handling goes beyond first-lesson material, though the core 'one backup regrows the whole tree' idea is novice-friendly; reviewer may downgrade if used for novice teaching. The source also contains test vectors, including known-invalid extended keys (useful for QA tooling); not excerpted.
